# Reclamation Day

## Language

**Balancer**:
The melancholy spirit embodied by the player, appearing when Earth reaches its environmental tipping point.

**Draining**:
The absorption of surrounding life into the Balancer, increasing its power and size.
_Avoid_: Harvesting

**Rebirth**:
The return of accumulated life to a drained region as new growth.
_Avoid_: Repair, resurrection
